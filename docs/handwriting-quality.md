# Quality

Evaluate content, geometry and style separately. Held-out evaluation measures actual VAE posterior XY reconstruction and stroke-mask accuracy, without updating weights or selecting checkpoints. Test has no atomic glyphs in the supplied corpus. Neither reconstruction loss nor label conditioning proves recognizer accuracy or perceptual style. Style/content remain independently unscored; supplied labels lack independent second review. See [model status](model-status.md) for measured results and limitations.
